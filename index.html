Frequently asked questions

Questions from employers

What if the candidate leaves?

Full recruitment includes a one-time free replacement if your new hire resigns, or is dismissed for proven incompetence or misconduct, within the replacement period: 30 days for Basic Outsourcing and Domestic Staff, and 60 days for percentage-based roles. Simply notify us in writing within that period. The Job Advert Package does not include a replacement.

How soon can we get staff?

Your shortlist typically arrives within 10–15 working days of receiving your payment and a complete job brief. If we cannot present a suitable candidate within 20 working days, we refund your fee less the non-refundable ₦50,000 advert fee.

Can I pay 50% before and 50% after?

Yes, for percentage-based recruitment: 50% before sourcing begins and the balance on or before the candidate's start date. Basic Outsourcing, Domestic Staff, the Job Advert Package and all Workforce Solutions are paid in full before work begins.

Who pays the new employee?

You do. You employ and pay your new staff directly. Our fee covers our recruitment services only, and we do not take any part of the employee's salary.

Do you charge candidates?

No. Candidates never pay us to be put forward for a role, so the people we present are there because they meet your requirements.

What do you need from us to get started?

A short job brief: the job title, duties, salary, location, working hours, start date and the experience or qualifications you need. We will help you shape it during your consultation.

Do you recruit across Nigeria?

Yes. We work fully online, so we can recruit for employers in Lagos, Abuja and every other state.

Do you have a physical office?

We operate as a fully virtual company, run from the United Kingdom, so there is no walk-in office. Our registered business address is in Lagos, and all consultations and interviews take place online, by WhatsApp, phone, Zoom or Google Meet.

Is there a minimum salary for roles?

Yes. We recruit for roles paying at least ₦50,000 per month.

Frequently asked questions

Questions from job seekers

Do I have to pay to get a job through you?

No. We do not charge registration fees, and we never take a cut of your salary. Employers pay us to recruit for them, so applying for our vacancies is completely free, as long as you meet the role's requirements.

Which of your services are paid?

Our CV writing, personal statements, packages, templates, guides and one-to-one career support are paid services. They are optional and completely separate from applying for our vacancies.

Will you search for jobs on my behalf?

No. We advertise the vacancies we are recruiting for on our platforms, and you can apply to any role that suits you. Our paid services help you prepare stronger applications.

Does buying a package guarantee me a job?

No. Our packages give you a stronger CV, better applications and confident interview preparation, which improve your chances. Hiring decisions are always made by the employer.

Where can I see your current vacancies?

We post our latest vacancies on Instagram and TikTok. Follow us so you never miss a role.

What if I can't make an interview?

Please tell us at least 24 hours in advance, so we can inform the employer and, where possible, arrange another time.

Do you have a physical office?

We operate as a fully virtual company, run from the United Kingdom, so there is no walk-in office. All applications, consultations and interviews take place online, by WhatsApp, phone, Zoom or Google Meet.
