How it works

From first call to your new hire

A clear, structured process, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Discovery call

    We learn about your business, the role and your working hours.

  2. Role brief

    We agree the duties, skills, schedule and salary range.

  3. Search and screening

    We source candidates and screen every application against your brief.

  4. Vetting

    Skills checks, video interviews and reference checks.

  5. Your shortlist

    You meet only the strongest candidates, with our honest notes.

  6. Hire and follow-up

    You make the offer, and we check in after your new hire starts.

Frequently asked questions

Questions from international employers

Who employs and pays the candidate?

You do. You hire the candidate directly and pay them directly, using whichever payment method suits you. Our fee is a one-time placement fee for the recruitment service.

Do you charge a monthly fee?

No. We charge a one-time placement fee based on the role. We don't add a markup to your hire's salary.

How do you vet candidates?

We screen every application against your brief, then run a skills check relevant to the role, a video interview and a reference check. You only meet candidates who pass.

What about time zones?

Nigeria is on West Africa Time, 5 to 6 hours ahead of US Eastern time depending on the season. Many of our candidates are happy to work hours that overlap with your business day. We agree the schedule with you at the start.

How long does it take?

It depends on the role, but most shortlists are ready within 2 to 3 weeks of agreeing the role brief.

Can you fill more than one role?

Yes. We can recruit for several roles at once. Tell us what you need and we'll put together a proposal for the full team.

Do candidates pay you?

No. Candidates never pay us to be put forward for a role, so the people we present are there because they meet your requirements.
